Keep your agent private

Your Studio is open to anyone who has its address. Lock it so only you can open it:

  1. In Vercel, open your project's Settings.
  2. Choose Deployment Protection.
  3. Under Vercel Authentication, turn on Require Log In and choose All Deployments.
  4. Click Save, then come back here.

It still looks open to anyone, or the Studio could not check. Check the steps above, then try again.
